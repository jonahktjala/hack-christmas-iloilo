Before you go

  • 01
    Join Discord before you leave on Friday

    There's no overnight stay at ICMAC. Your team keeps building online through the night at ndgt.us/discord.

  • 02
    Get home safe, then hack

    Travel home with a buddy where you can and tell your family where you are. Rest is part of the plan. A tired team makes slower work.

  • 03
    Be back at ICMAC by 9:00 AM Saturday

    We start the day together with devotions at 9:00.

  • 04
    Save to the cloud, often

    Keep your files in one shared folder your whole team can open. Don't let a dead laptop end your project.

  • 05
    Use what you have the right to use

    Stick to your own work or royalty-free music, images and fonts, and credit your sources. Borrowed content can get a post taken down.

  • 06
    Create with care

    Ask before filming or photographing anyone. Not everyone here follows Jesus yet, so welcome every question and every teammate.

  • 07
    Charge everything

    Bring your chargers and earphones on both days.
